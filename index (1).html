Privacy Policy

How Lucerna handles PDF documents and information when you use the app.

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Lucerna (the “App”), an Android application for creating and viewing PDF documents. It describes the App’s intended handling of documents, permissions, and information.

1. Documents and local processing

Lucerna is designed to create and view PDF documents on your device. An internet connection is not required for its core PDF creation and viewing functionality. Documents are saved in locations provided or selected by you, subject to Android’s storage features and the App’s implementation.

The App’s core PDF functionality is designed for local document handling and does not require uploading PDFs to a Lucerna-operated cloud service. A PDF may contain personal or sensitive information depending on what you put in it.

2. Personal information and accounts

The core PDF functionality does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to the developer. The developer does not intentionally collect the contents of locally stored PDFs through the App’s core offline functionality.

This describes the intended core functionality; the actual released build and included libraries must be checked for any additional data collection or processing. If any component collects information, this policy must be updated to describe it accurately.

3. Permissions and files

Depending on the Android version and actions you take, Lucerna may use Android’s document picker or related file-access capabilities to open, create, save, or export PDFs. Access is subject to Android’s permission system and the access you grant.

You can manage supported permissions in Android settings. Files remain subject to the storage location and settings where you save them and can be managed using the appropriate file-management tools.

4. Sharing and third-party apps

If you choose to share or export a PDF using another app, service, or recipient, you direct that transfer. The receiving app or service may access, transmit, or retain the document under its own terms and privacy policy. Review those practices before sharing confidential information.

If a future version introduces online services, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other third-party components that process information, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant practices.

5. Data retention and deletion

PDF files you save remain in the selected device or storage location until you delete them or the relevant storage system removes them. Delete saved files using the App, where supported, or the applicable file-management tools.

This policy does not describe a separate server-side retention period for PDF documents because the App’s core functionality is designed for local handling rather than developer-operated cloud storage.

6. Security

Local processing can reduce the need to transmit documents to remote services, but it does not guarantee security in every circumstance. Security also depends on your device, access controls, storage location, backups, and any app or recipient with which you share a file. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

The App’s intended audience and applicable Google Play Families requirements should be assessed separately. The App should not be used to collect children’s personal information in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised if Lucerna’s features, permissions, technical components, or data practices change. The current version will be published at this webpage, with the effective date updated when appropriate.
